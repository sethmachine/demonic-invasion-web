[size=18][color=#E8A000]W[/color][/size][size=13][color=#E8A000]hat is Daemonia Epic?[/color][/size]

Daemonia Epic is a cooperative-competitive experience set in the age of fantasy, magic, swords, and demons. Players control a Magister — a powerful wizard-lord — as part of a greater Empire, each representing one of 5 rival kingdoms. Players must work together to complete quests, build up their kingdom, foster diplomacy, and explore Hell to power up their hero, all while combating the ever-growing demonic invasion. This game combines elements of RTS, RPG, and Diplomacy all in one. As players succeed or fail Quests, the demons grow stronger, pressuring everyone to cooperate before it is too late.

[color=#CC0000][b]Players[/b][/color]: 2–5
